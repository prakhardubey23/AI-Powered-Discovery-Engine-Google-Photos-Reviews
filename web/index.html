Exploring User Struggles in Photo Retrieval

💡
Key Takeaway

Users mainly struggle to retrieve people and old scanned photos because they remember contextual cues like people, places, activities, time of the day, life periods but not exact dates or metadata. When search fails, they simplify queries or switch to manual scrolling, and after 2–3 unsuccessful attempts especially without refinement guidance they often abandon retrieval.
